ART, CURIOSITY & A LITTLE DATA

Every eye sees
something different.

CAC Art Lab asks which of two paintings you prefer. There is no right answer. After a round of up to ten choices, you can compare your preferences with precomputed AI judgments and explore the artworks you chose.

Before you choose: every choice, including a skip, is sent to the online study database. The next pair appears only after the save is confirmed. If you do not want a choice saved, please do not take part. Mood, interests and language settings are optional and do not select the artworks you see.

What is in the collection?

The gallery contains 5,326 verified public-domain records that The Metropolitan Museum of Art classifies as Paintings. The choice deck has 2,860 pairs: 532 original pairs and 2,328 new pairs. A visual screen removed 194 pairs whose images were unsuitable for compact two-image choices; those artworks remain in the gallery. Pairs are sampled across three date ranges, the left and right positions are balanced where possible, and a pair will not repeat until the deck is exhausted.

Date ranges do not guarantee matching subject matter, culture, religion or artistic tradition. The visitors who choose to participate are not a representative sample of the public.

Your view and the AI

The original pairs use the supplied AI-judge and feature-model results. Every active new pair has votes from the same six AI-judge configurations and a feature-model prediction from a numerical reproduction of the original method. The reproduction matched the saved old predictions to four decimal places, but its model fingerprint was not identical. No AI service is called while you choose. Agreement measures shared preferences, not accuracy or objective beauty.

A short round observation may describe brightness, saturation, detail or symmetry. These are image measurements, not explanations of why you or an AI system preferred an artwork.

How choices are saved

Each saved event includes random browser and round IDs, the dataset and pair IDs, left/right order, choice or skip, round position, client timestamps and elapsed time, a short source label, and any optional mood, interests or language you entered. The server adds its receipt time. Duplicate retries use the same event ID. Browser progress also stays on this device. Starting over does not erase records already received online.

The app database does not store names, email addresses, raw IP addresses or precise locations. Cloudflare may process IP addresses in network and service logs. Collected votes can include repeat participation, so browser IDs and vote counts are not verified numbers of distinct people.

Artwork credits

Images and artwork information come from The Met Open Access. An online collection record does not mean an artwork is currently on view.
