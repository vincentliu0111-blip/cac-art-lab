ART, CURIOSITY & A LITTLE DATA

Every eye sees
something different.

Follow Your Eye asks which of two paintings you prefer. There is no right answer. After a round of up to ten choices, you can compare your preferences with precomputed AI judgments and explore the artworks you chose.

Before you choose: every choice, including a skip, and an approximate country or region inferred from your connection are sent to the online study database. The next pair appears only after the save is confirmed. If you do not want them saved, please do not take part. Mood, interests and language settings are optional and do not select the artworks you see.

What is in the collection?

The gallery contains 5,326 verified public-domain records that The Metropolitan Museum of Art classifies as Paintings. The evaluated catalog has 2,751 pairs: 478 original pairs and 2,273 new pairs. Two image reviews removed 303 pairs whose display images were unsuitable for compact two-image choices; those artworks remain in the gallery. The experience draws from 1,200 existing pairs with the same broad medium and similar measured brightness, saturation, detail and symmetry. This practical filter reduces abrupt contrasts such as ink against tempera; it does not establish that two works have the same art-historical style. New ten-pair rounds generally include seven pairs in which both works belong to Western-focused Met curatorial departments, with three from the remaining screened pairs. The first three positions draw from 14 manually reviewed Western-department pairs while unused ones remain. Pairs are sampled across three date ranges, the left and right positions are balanced where possible, and a pair will not repeat until the selected deck is exhausted.

The gallery still includes every work. Its initial browse order shows Western-department works more often, while search can find any work directly. Department is a practical display grouping based on The Met's metadata, not a complete statement about an artwork's culture or identity. Records without confirmed department information are not counted as Western for this purpose.

Date ranges do not guarantee matching subject matter, culture, religion or artistic tradition. The visitors who choose to participate are not a representative sample of the public.

Your view and the AI

The original pairs use the supplied AI-judge and feature-model results. Every active new pair has votes from the same six AI-judge configurations and a feature-model prediction from a numerical reproduction of the original method. The reproduction matched the saved old predictions to four decimal places, but its model fingerprint was not identical. No AI service is called while you choose. Agreement measures shared preferences, not accuracy or objective beauty.

A short round observation may describe brightness, saturation, detail or symmetry. These are image measurements, not explanations of why you or an AI system preferred an artwork.

A CMU professor suggested adding a chance-adjusted comparison. Codex assisted with the analysis code and website display. Kappa adjusts for agreement expected from each side’s overall A/B choices. The labels “slight,” “fair” and “moderate” are conventions, not grades. The visitor intervals resample anonymous browser sessions, which are not verified people. Many choices in this snapshot arrived in a short period, and some sessions have timing records inconsistent with the site's normal one-at-a-time flow. Those records are not removed because of timing: the live count includes every saved non-skip A/B choice, and each visitor kappa includes all choices eligible for that comparison (excluding skips and exact prediction ties). The records do not establish whether each session was an independent person or what caused the timing discrepancy. Agreement says nothing about which choice is right. Kappa and its interval are a dated snapshot; only the saved-choice count above updates live.

How choices are saved

Each saved event includes random browser and round IDs, the dataset and pair IDs, left/right order, choice or skip, round position, client timestamps and elapsed time, a short source label, any optional mood, interests or language you entered, and an approximate country or region inferred by Cloudflare from your connection. The country may be unknown or inaccurate, for example when using a VPN. The server adds its receipt time. Duplicate retries use the same event ID. Browser progress also stays on this device. Starting over does not erase records already received online.

The app database does not store names, email addresses, raw IP addresses or precise locations. The study owner can view the country alongside each choice; public vote totals do not show it. Choices saved before this update have no country information. Cloudflare may process IP addresses in network and service logs. Collected votes can include repeat participation, so browser IDs and vote counts are not verified numbers of distinct people.

Artwork credits

Images and artwork information come from The Met Open Access. An online collection record does not mean an artwork is currently on view.

FOLLOW YOUR EYE · 中文导览

每个人看画的方式，都有点不一样。

两幅画摆在面前，你会更喜欢哪一幅？凭直觉选就好，没有标准答案。每轮最多选 10 组，也可以跳过。结束后，你可以看看自己的选择与预先算好的 AI 偏好有哪些相同、哪些不同。

开始前，先说清楚

每次选择或跳过，连同 Cloudflare 根据网络连接推断的粗略国家或地区，都会保存到在线数据库；保存成功后才会出现下一组。如果不希望这些信息被保存，请不要参与选画。心情、兴趣和语言设置都可以不填；填写后会随选择记录保存，也不会决定你看到哪些画。

画作从哪里来？

画廊收录了大都会艺术博物馆 5,326 幅已核验、属于公有领域的绘画作品。已评测题库有 2,751 组画对，其中 478 组来自原题库，2,273 组是后来增加的。两轮图像检查剔除了 303 组不适合并排观看的画对，但相关作品仍留在画廊中。

选画体验从中使用 1,200 组：它们的大类媒材相同，画面的明暗、色彩、细节和对称程度也比较接近。这样能减少明显的视觉落差，却不能据此断定两幅画属于同一种艺术史风格。

每轮通常有 7 组来自大都会以西方艺术为重点的策展部门，另外 3 组来自其余通过筛选的画对。开头 3 组会优先从 14 组人工复看过的画对中抽取。画廊只是让这类作品较早出现，所有 5,326 幅画仍可浏览和搜索。这里的“西方”是依据馆方部门资料作出的展示分组，并不能概括一件作品的全部文化背景。

AI 在这里做什么？

原题库沿用已有的 AI 评审和图像特征模型结果；新增画对也取得相同六套 AI 评审配置的结果。图像特征预测按原方法重新计算：旧结果核对到小数点后四位一致，但模型文件的指纹并不相同。你选画时不会实时调用 AI。

结果里的“一致”只表示你和 AI 在这些画对上作出了相同选择，不代表判断正确，也不是美的客观分数。页面提到的亮度、饱和度、细节或对称性是图像测量值，不能解释你为什么喜欢一幅画。参与者也并非随机抽样；票数不等于独立人数，更不代表大众审美。

加入扣除碰巧一致的比较，来自一位 CMU 教授的建议；统计程序和网站展示由 Codex 协助实现。Kappa 会按双方总体 A/B 选择比例计算预期的一致。“轻微、一般、中等”只是常用约定，不是成绩。访客区间按匿名浏览器会话重新抽样，会话不等于已核实的人。本次快照有大量选择集中在短时间内，部分会话的时间记录与网站正常逐题保存流程不一致。这些记录不会因时间异常被剔除：实时总数计入所有已保存的非跳过 A/B 选择；访客 κ 计入各项比较中符合条件的全部选择（跳过与预测恰好打平者不计入该项 κ）。记录无法证明每个会话都来自独立真人，也无法确定时间差异的成因。这个指标也不能判断谁选得对。Kappa 和区间仍是定期快照，只有上方的已保存选择数实时更新。

你的选择如何保存？

每条记录包含随机生成的浏览器和轮次编号、画对与数据版本、左右位置、选择或跳过、题目序号、时间与耗时、简短来源标记，以及你自愿填写的心情、兴趣和语言。服务器还会记录接收时间。浏览进度也保存在当前设备；重新开始不会删除已经传到线上的记录。

国家或地区可能无法判断，也可能因 VPN 等原因不准确；旧记录无法补出地区。站主能看到每条记录的地区，公开票数不会显示。应用数据库不保存姓名、邮箱、原始 IP 地址或精确位置，但 Cloudflare 的网络与服务日志可能处理 IP 地址。

作品来源

图片和作品资料来自大都会艺术博物馆开放资源。作品能在线浏览，不代表它目前正在馆内展出。
