YOUR CHOICE

How choices are saved

Each choice, including a skip, is saved online before the next pair appears. If you do not want a choice saved, please do not take part.

The study saves random browser and round IDs, the pair and display order, your choice, time spent, a short source label, any optional settings you provide, and an approximate country or region inferred by Cloudflare from your connection. The country may be unknown or inaccurate, for example when using a VPN.

The app database does not store your name, email, raw IP address, or precise location. The country is visible to the study owner with each choice, but is not shown in public vote totals. Earlier choices have no country information. Cloudflare may process IP addresses in its network and service logs. No AI service is called when you choose.

Previously received choices remain in the database after you close or restart the page. Contact the study owner to request removal.

These votes are a convenience sample and do not represent the general public.
